Case study: BNPL for cross-border trade.

01

Suppliers paid promptly

Designed so the supplier gets settled the same day the order is confirmed — no waiting on the buyer's payment cycle.

02

Buyers pay later

Buyers would get 30–90 day terms at checkout, so cash stays in their business while goods are already moving.

03

AI does the underwriting

Trade and behavioral signals are meant to feed an automated risk model that returns a credit decision in under a second.

Embedded BNPL checkout

A checkout flow buyers already understand, designed to integrate directly into a marketplace or ordering platform.

Real-time risk monitoring

Planned ongoing portfolio monitoring to flag emerging risk early, so terms can adjust before exposure grows.
